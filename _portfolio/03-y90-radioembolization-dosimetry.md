This pipeline turns a computed blood-flow field into a patient-specific absorbed-dose map for Yttrium-90 radioembolization, a targeted therapy for liver tumors.

It takes the CFD or PINN flow split, distributes radioactive microspheres, and convolves their deposition with a dose-point kernel to produce a three-dimensional absorbed-dose map in gray (Gy).

*The code is private and available on request.*
