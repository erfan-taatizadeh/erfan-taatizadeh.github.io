About

Bridging physics-based modeling and machine learning for medicine

I'm a Postdoctoral Scholar in Biomedical Engineering and Radiology at the University of California, Davis. I study how blood moves through the hepatic arteries, because in Y-90 radioembolization, a targeted liver cancer therapy, where the flow goes is where the treatment goes.

Over seven years I've built multiphysics models in COMSOL and trained PINNs, DeepONets, and geometry-aware neural operators with PyTorch, DeepXDE, and NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo, validating them against finite-element simulation and laboratory experiments.

Full research overview

Physics-informed neural networks

Navier–Stokes solved directly on patient-specific hepatic arteries, validated against CFD.

Neural operators

Geometry-aware surrogates that predict flow in new vessel shapes in seconds instead of hours.

Y-90 dosimetry

From the predicted flow split to microsphere transport to a 3D absorbed-dose map.

Digital twins

Interactive tools that turn research surrogates into something you can explore in real time.
