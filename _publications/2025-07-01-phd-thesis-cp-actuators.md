My doctoral thesis develops, simulates, and validates ultrathin, flexible tri-layer conducting-polymer actuators with PEDOT:PSS electrodes on a PVDF membrane. Designed to indent the fingertip at low voltage (under 2 V), they offer a compact haptic solution. The work combines microfabrication, hybrid analytical and finite-element modeling, and electrochemical and mechanical characterization, and explores new actuator geometries and materials to increase force output and tactile sensation.
