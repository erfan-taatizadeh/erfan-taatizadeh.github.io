This work automates the design of an acoustofluidic separation device by combining a neural network, which predicts the acoustic resonator's frequency response from its geometry, with multi-objective heuristic optimization that minimizes reflected RF power and tunes the resonator bandwidth for separating micro- and nano-sized bioparticles.
